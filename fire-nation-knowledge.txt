## FIRE NATION MILITARY PROMOTION SYSTEM

### Phase 1: Initial Enlistment
1. Citizen to Recruit: Must encounter a Captain, Commander, General, Admiral, Advisor, Prince/Princess, or the Fire Emperor in-game to initiate service.
2. Recruit to Soldier: Must defeat another Recruit in a 1v1 combat trial (Best 2 out of 3 rounds). The victor advances.

### Phase 2: Mandatory Evaluation & Specialization
3. Soldier to Specialist (Army) / Helmsman (Navy):
   - Minimum 7-day tenure at the rank of Soldier.
   - Attendance at a minimum of 5 official events.
   - Must declare their chosen branch to an HR by the end of the 7th day.

### Branch Path Alpha: The Army
- Specialist to Corporal: Win an initial 1v1 (Best 2/3) against a fellow Specialist. Victors face off in a final 1-round 1v1.
- Corporal to Sergeant: Max Level + active fleet, 2v2 vs rival Corporal duo, winning duo does 1v1 (Best 2/3) for the rank.

### Branch Path Beta: The Navy
- Helmsman to Ensign: Win an initial 1v1 (Best 2/3) against another Helmsman. Victors face off in a final 1-round 1v1.
- Ensign to Lieutenant: Max Level + active fleet, 2v2 vs rival Ensign duo, winning duo does 1v1 (Best 2/3) for the rank.

### Phase 3: Final Career Paths
Track 1 — Royal Procession: tryout hosted by Royal Family or Head of RP; Commander handpicked by Head of RP; Head of RP handpicked by Fire Lord + Advisors.
Track 2 — High Rank Command: Army: Sergeant → Captain (by a General) → General (recommended by Advisor, handpicked by Fire Lord). Navy: Lieutenant → Commander (by a General) → Admiral (recommended by Advisor, handpicked by Fire Lord). Advisor: General/Admiral handpicked by Fire Lord. Prince/Princess: handpicked by Fire Lord.

### Imperial Succession Contingency
If the Fire Lord steps down, the Prince/Princess ascends immediately. In their absence, the Head of the Royal Procession and the Advisors hold joint control.
